A topic starts with its number in square brackets. Anything after the
number on that line is a label for you and is not shipped. Each blank-line
separated paragraph below it is the next hint level, so a topic reads as a
nudge, then a stronger nudge, then the answer.

[0] Getting started

Try looking at things, and taking anything that is not nailed down.

The lamp is the one object you cannot finish without. Take it before you
go anywhere dark.

[1] Verbs to try

Stop the music with MUTE, play it again with MUSIC

Play a sound with SAMPLE or ZAP

Play a video with MOVIE or REEL

Look at the clock with TIME

Blink with BLINK
